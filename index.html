Most of the places have already gone, quietly, within our circle. A few remain. One of them could be yours.

Now, the rough part

We will be honest, because we like you.

This is a working farm, not a resort. Mud on your shoes, insects at the lamp, a cold stream around your ankles, and a bed that is clean and simple, not five-star.

The Feni Trail is on the tougher side. Come for that.
